Conference Passport — Virtual Demo Checklist

40-person staff dry run · one page · you drive, they play along on their phones

Attendee site gentle-flower-01d10d50f.7.azurestaticapps.net Admin: …/admin

1 Before you go live T-10 min

  • Open the attendee site once yourself (warms the server — kills the cold-start pause)
  • Deactivate the “test” sponsor (Admin → Sponsors → toggle Off)
  • Log in on your phone as a known-good example
  • Tab A: the QR code / link to share
  • Tab B: Admin → Dashboard (your live scoreboard)
  • Mute notifications on the machine you’re sharing

2 Opening 60 sec

  • What it is “A game to get attendees to actually talk to our sponsors — not walk past them.”
  • How it works “Collect a stamp at each table by answering a question only their rep can tell you. Fill your passport → you’re in the prize drawing.”
  • Their job “You’re about to do exactly what 160 attendees will do in October. Scan the code and follow along.”

3 Live walkthrough — everyone does it with you 6–8 min

  • Scan / tap the link → name + email. “This is your identity for the whole conference.”
  • Check email, tap the link. “Came through in seconds, from a real address, with our logo — that’s deliberate.”
  • 3-card intro (first-timers only) — what it is · how stops work · how you win
  • Passport home — points bar, rank badge, “Next Stop.” “Their home base all week.”
  • Tap a sponsor → get the answer WRONG on purpose. “Watch — it doesn’t lock me out. It nudges me to go talk to the rep. That’s the whole point.”
  • Answer right → stamp + points animation
  • Complete several → completion celebration → “you’re in the drawing”

4 The closer — flip to Dashboard 2 min

  • “Everything you just did is showing up here live — this is what staff see day-of.”
  • Point at: Registered climbing · Check-ins today · Live feed with their names
  • Sponsor tables — which are hot, which show “No visits”
  • Almost there — people one stop from finishing
  • Export tab: “At the end I click one button and get the prize-drawing list.”

5 If something hiccups

  • A few emails lag 30–60 s “40 of you hit send in the same minute — it paces them. Every link still works. Real attendees trickle in.”
  • “I didn’t get it” → check spam once, then move on. Don’t chase one person live.
  • Red “couldn’t send” message → that’s the app being honest, not silent. Note the name; check after.
  • Wrong-answer confusion → point to the “go ask at the table” nudge. It’s intended.

6 Close — ask for what you need 1 min

  • “Two things: was the flow obvious? and did the sponsor questions make sense?”
  • “Drop any confusion in the chat — that’s what makes October smooth.”
  • Thank them; tell them their passports stay live so they can poke around after
