Conference Passport App
IPELRA Staff Checklist

Everything IPELRA staff needs to provide, confirm, or decide before the app can go live. Items marked Required must be completed before launch.

How this works: This checklist is for the IPELRA team only. A separate sheet goes to each sponsor. Once all items below are checked off and sponsor info is collected, we handle the rest — no technical work required from your end.

1 · Branding & Logos
Official IPELRA logo file Required
PNG or SVG file, transparent background preferred. Used in the app and in every email sent to attendees. A transparent background ensures it looks correct on all screen colors. (A screenshot from the website may work but can appear blurry on high-resolution phone screens.)
2 · People & Access
Admin email addresses Required
The email address(es) for each person who needs access to the admin portal (dashboard, attendee list, export, etc.). These people will receive a login link by email — no password needed. Typically 2–4 people.
3 · Sponsor Information
Final sponsor list with tiers Required
A complete list of all participating exhibitors and whether each is Gold or Platinum tier. This determines point values (Gold = 100 pts, Platinum = 150 pts). Use the Sponsor Information Sheet to collect details from each company.
Completed sponsor info sheets returned Required
Each sponsor returns their filled-out info sheet to you. You forward everything to us at once. See the companion document: Sponsor Information Sheet.
4 · Conference Details to Confirm
Prize station location at Eagle Ridge Confirm
The app currently directs completers to "the Prize Station near the conference registration desk." Please confirm this is accurate — if the prize station will be somewhere else, let us know before launch so we can update the wording.
Passport open/close dates and times Confirm
Currently set to open October 5, 2026 and lock October 7, 2026 (end of conference). If either date or time needs to change, let us know.
Prize & drawing process Confirm
The app tracks every attendee who completes the passport. We can export a CSV of completers for a prize drawing. Confirm what the prize is and how you plan to run the drawing so we can make sure the app wording matches.
Review the Help & FAQ page Confirm
The app has a built-in help section that answers common attendee questions (how to earn points, what to do if you forget your link, etc.). We'll share the draft text — please read through it and flag anything that doesn't match how you plan to run the event.
5 · During the Conference — Staff Duties
Print QR codes for each sponsor booth
We will generate a QR code card for each sponsor. Print one per booth and set it on the table. Sponsors don't need to do anything technical — attendees scan the QR code to get to that sponsor's page in the app.
Activate the passport in the admin portal on opening day
The passport starts in "off" mode. On the morning of October 5, log into the admin portal and flip the passport to Live. This allows attendees to start checking in.
Monitor the admin dashboard during the conference
The dashboard shows real-time check-ins, completions, and flagged activity. Typically no action needed — just a good way to see how engagement is going.
Help attendees who say they didn't get their login email
Direct them to the login page and have them request a new link. Links expire in 15 minutes — if they waited too long, they just request another one. No password, no account to reset.
6 · After the Conference
Export completed attendees list for prize drawing
In the admin portal, use the Export page to download a CSV of all attendees who completed the passport. Use this list for your prize drawing.
Run Conference Reset to archive data Optional
The admin portal includes a Reset function that archives all attendee and check-in data from this year before the next conference. Not urgent — just do it when you're ready to clean up for next year.
