3 · Your Passport Question & Answer
How this works:

Attendees visit your booth, have a brief conversation with your team, and then answer your question in the app to earn points. The app accepts minor spelling mistakes automatically, so attendees don't need to type perfectly.

Your Question Required
The question attendees will see in the app after visiting your booth. It should be answerable after a 1–2 minute conversation with your team — not a trivia question, but something that naturally comes up when you describe your company.
Good example: "What type of software does [Company] specialize in?"
Also good: "What is the name of [Company]'s flagship product?"
Avoid: Trivia, trick questions, or anything with multiple possible correct answers.
Correct Answer Keyword(s) Required
The key word or phrase in the correct answer. The app uses this to check whether attendees typed the right thing — it does not have to match exactly, but it needs to be specific enough to distinguish correct answers from wrong ones.

This is never shown to attendees upfront. After 3 failed attempts, the app shows a partial hint (first letter of each word only).
Example question: "What type of software does CityTech specialize in?"
Example keyword: permitting software

The app would accept: "permitting software," "Permitting Software," "permitng software" (minor typo), etc.

How to Return This Sheet

Email your completed answers and logo file to your IPELRA contact by September 1, 2026. If you have questions about what to put for the question/answer section, reach out — we're happy to help you pick something that works well in the app.
